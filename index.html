How to Command

You are a general of the Central Powers. You never control a single soldier — you spawn, position, supply and lead whole formations.

Resources

⚔ Manpower and ⛽ Supply pay for every unit. They regenerate over time (Germany fastest).

Deploying troops

Click a unit card (bottom) or press 1–9. Units appear at your HQ and move up the active lane. Click the battlefield or use ▲/▼ to pick the active lane.

Orders

Advance · Hold (man the trench) · Fall back. Engineers (E) upgrade the trench; W lays barbed wire.

Special Ability

The big central button charges over time. Press R when it glows to unleash your faction power (German Support, Industrial Surge, Artillery Barrage, Mountain Hold, Desert Raid).

Watch out for

Tanks (German troops fear them until you field your own armour) · naval bombardment · weather · morale & desertion · front-specific events.

Win / Lose

Defence fronts: survive the timer. Offence/Mixed: smash the enemy HQ. You lose if your HQ falls. Space = pause.
